Privacy policy

Reserve Signal Uploader · last updated 29 September 2026

What the app is

Reserve Signal Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Reserve Signal YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own YouTube account, with their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and publishing time. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • With the owner's consent, the app receives an OAuth authorization for the scopes needed to upload and manage the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl).
  • It reads the signed-in channel's ID and title, only to confirm it is uploading to the Reserve Signal channel.
  • It does not read, collect or store any other user's data, comments, subscribers, watch history or analytics.

How data is stored and shared

  • The authorization token is stored only in the owner's local macOS Keychain, on the owner's computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Uploaded videos and their metadata are the channel's own content, published under YouTube's terms.

Revoking access

The authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Keychain entry removes the local copy.
